Which number goes with which sign?

HT 12 is a clay tablet from Hagia Triada on Crete.

What the writer put on the clay

Find the line with QA-TI-DA-TE in the drawing below. After those four signs come a single sign called A608 and the number 5. Farther down, QE-TU-NE comes before the sign A510 and the number 1.

What it might mean

Perhaps the longer groups name accounts and the single signs identify what was counted. Another tablet with A608 or A510 beside a different account could help test that idea. In this reading, the separate sign E goes with 30, and DA-I goes with 50.
