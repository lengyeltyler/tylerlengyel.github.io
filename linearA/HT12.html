HT 12 · Which sign carries the quantity?

What the tablet records

QA-TI-DA-TE precedes A608 and 5. Farther down, QE-TU-NE precedes A510 and 1. The separate entry E carries 30. DA-I appears beside 50.

Interpretation and test

The two longer sign groups may identify an account or party while the following signs specify items. A second document pairing the same item signs with different labels would support this distinction. The quantity 30 belongs with E in this edition.
