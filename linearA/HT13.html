A broken line in a wine list

HT 13 is a clay tablet from Hagia Triada on Crete.

What the writer put on the clay

A sign for wine appears at the top. One line reads TE-TU 56. At the bottom, the writer put KU-RO beside 130 and a sign researchers usually read as one half.

What it might mean

The whole numbers add to 130. A half beside TE-KI would bring the sum to 130½, exactly the total at the bottom. One line, RE-ZA, is damaged. If it held another fraction, the numbers would no longer match.
