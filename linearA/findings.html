What the 71.36% Means

The current score is a structural confidence estimate: it measures whether sign order, recurrence, normalization, model fit, and stress tests point to repeatable organization. It is not a translation confidence score and it does not establish phonetic readings or meanings.

The current evidence does not justify raising the claim to 85%. The strongest parts of the case are data coverage and normalization stability; the weakest parts are predictive template strength, out-of-sample certainty, and the lack of independent semantic anchors.
