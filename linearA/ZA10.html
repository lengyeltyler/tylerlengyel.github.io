One extra sign earns a separate entry

ZA 10 is a clay tablet from Zakros on Crete.

What the writer put on the clay

On side A, TA-NA-TE has 2 beside it. Add an A at the start and A-TA-NA-TE gets its own entry, with 1. Another pair, A-KU-MI-NA and A-DU-KU-MI-NA, differs by the sign DU. Each gets 1.

What it might mean

The writer kept these spellings separate. They might name different people, or the extra sign might change the meaning of a word. To test the second idea, look for other pairs where adding the same sign changes how the word is used. Side B also has a wine sign and repeats the label DU-RE-ZA-SE from side A.
