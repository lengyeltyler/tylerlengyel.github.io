ZA 10 · Related spellings entered separately

What the tablet records

On side A, TA-NA-TE has 2 and A-TA-NA-TE has 1. A-KU-MI-NA and A-DU-KU-MI-NA each have 1. Side B includes the wine sign and repeats DU-RE-ZA-SE from side A.

Interpretation and test

The separate entries make it risky to normalize away initial A or internal DU. The forms could identify distinct people or related words. To test whether A has a grammatical function, look for the same alternation across multiple stems in comparable contexts.
