A total you can check

HT 9 is a clay tablet from Hagia Triada on Crete.

What the writer put on the clay

On side B, the writer listed 3, 3, 8, 2, 2, 2 and 4. The next line reads KU-RO 24. The seven numbers add to exactly 24.

What it might mean

This is one reason researchers read KU-RO as “total.” Six of the labels also appear on side A, in a different order and with some different amounts. The writer may have used one list to keep track of two sets of payments or goods.
