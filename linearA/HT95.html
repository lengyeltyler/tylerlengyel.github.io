Six labels on both sides

HT 95 is a clay tablet from Hagia Triada on Crete.

What the writer put on the clay

Side B starts with A-DU. Each of the six labels below it gets 10. The same labels appear on side A. Four still get 10, but SA-RU gets 20 and QE-RA₂-U gets 7. That makes 60 on side B and 67 on side A.

What it might mean

One side might show the amounts expected and the other the amounts actually handed over. We do not know who gave or received them. On a different tablet, HT 86, two entries under A-DU get 20 each, so the heading can go with amounts other than ten.
