# HT 13 · A wine account with a damaged entry

The heading includes the wine sign. TE-TU is followed by 56, and the closing entry reads KU-RO 130 plus the sign conventionally read as one half. The six whole-number parts sum to 130.

If this list balances exactly and the other entries have been read correctly, the half beside TE-KI accounts for the entire fraction in the total. A possible additional fraction at the damaged RE-ZA entry would leave a discrepancy. The break deserves to stay visible.
