# HT 95 · The same six labels on both faces

On side B, A-DU heads six entries of 10. Side A assigns four of those labels 10 again, but SA-RU has 20 and QE-RA₂-U has 7. The sums of the preserved amounts are 60 and 67 respectively.

A fixed assessment compared with an actual allocation is one possible explanation. On HT 86a, DA-ME and MI-NU-TE follow A-DU with 20 each. That comparison rules out a general rule that A-DU requires ten. The direction of the transactions remains open.
