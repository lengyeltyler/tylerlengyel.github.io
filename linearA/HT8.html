One label, two amounts

HT 8 is a clay tablet from Hagia Triada on Crete.

What the writer put on the clay

The same label appears on both sides. Researchers write it as QA-*310-I. The number after the star stands for a sign whose sound is uncertain.

What it might mean

On one side, a fraction follows the label. On the other, a 1 and fraction signs follow it. The writer may have recorded two amounts for the same person or thing. A tablet with clearer headings could tell us what the two amounts were for.
