HT 8 · Fractional entries on two faces

What the tablet records

QA-*310-I appears with a fractional sign on side A and with 1 and fractional signs on side B. The asterisk identifies a catalogued sign with an uncertain sound.

Interpretation and test

The repetition suggests that both faces track the same entity or category under different amounts. Comparing the headings on another intact record could tell us what distinguishes the transactions.
