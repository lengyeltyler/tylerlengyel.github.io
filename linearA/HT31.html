HT 31 · An inventory of vessels

What the tablet records

A cup-shaped sign accompanies SU-PA₃-RA 300 and PA-TA-QE 3,000. Other entries show different vessel outlines and much smaller counts.

Interpretation and test

PA-TA-QE plausibly identifies a type of small handleless cup. Another inventory using the word with a large jar would weaken that proposal. Shape, capacity, material and contents remain possible distinctions.
