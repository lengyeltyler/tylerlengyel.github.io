A drawing beside a word

HT 31 is a clay tablet from Hagia Triada on Crete.

What the writer put on the clay

The writer drew a small cup without a handle beside PA-TA-QE and the number 3,000. A similar cup appears beside SU-PA₃-RA and 300. Other pots on the list have different outlines and much smaller counts.

What it might mean

PA-TA-QE may name a kind of cup. The two similar drawings leave open what the words describe. Size, material or use could tell the cups apart. Finding PA-TA-QE beside a large jar on another tablet would weaken the cup idea.
