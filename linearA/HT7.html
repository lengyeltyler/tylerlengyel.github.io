Five entries add up to ten

HT 7 is a clay tablet from Hagia Triada on Crete.

What the writer put on the clay

The first line reads QE-TI. Below it are five entries, including I-RU-JA 3 and DU-JA 4. Add all five numbers and you get ten. That is our calculation.

What it might mean

Two labels turn up elsewhere. DA-RE has 1 here and 2 on the back of HT 10. TE-TU has 1 here and 56 on HT 13. These could be names or places that different scribes kept track of.
