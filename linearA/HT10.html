Why write a label twice?

HT 10 is a clay tablet from Hagia Triada on Crete.

What the writer put on the clay

DA-RE appears beside 16 and a fraction on one side and beside 2 on the other. Another label, U-*325-ZA, appears twice on the first side. The star and number stand for a sign with an uncertain sound.

What it might mean

Next to the repeated label, one amount is 4. The other starts with 14 where the clay is damaged. The writer gave each amount its own entry. Combining them would hide that choice.
