HT 10 · DA-RE across two accounts

What the tablet records

DA-RE is followed by 16 and a fractional sign on side A and by 2 on side B. U-*325-ZA occurs twice on side A, beside 4 and a damaged entry beginning with 14.

Interpretation and test

The scribe could list a label more than once on the same face. A reconstruction that automatically merges repeated labels risks losing separate transactions.
