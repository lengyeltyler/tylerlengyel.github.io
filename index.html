    Current Focus

    For the past two years I've been working on a project I call 'Phil' which ultimately is an Art project that will live on Ethereum forever. The more technical side, Phil is a Generative Art project where the data lives 100% on-chain, meaning that as long as Ethereum is alive, Phil will be as well. The even more technical side is Phil uses Zero Knowledge Technology to allow the user minting to remain completely anonymous if they so choose. The user will go to the mint site for Phil which represents something very similar to that of the Avastars NFT project, allowing you to scroll through countless combinations of Layers that I created which when combined together, form a complete and final image of Phil. When you land on a generation you connect with, you'll be able to mint, which is when the frontend triggers the backend to create a Smart Account, sending the NFT to the newly created Smart Account with your initial wallet being one of the signers, and the STARK proof becoming a form of Two-Factor authentication. This STARK Proof will allow you to be the gatekeeper of your assets, using the STARK proof as a final signer or nothing at all. The decision will be yours.

    The project itself started as a meme token that I was going to have be used to mint/claim Phils, but like everything else in life, it grew legs and became something bigger and better than originally thought. The time is coming where I will finally release the project consisting of 369 total Phils, allowing for 1 Phil to be minted every 36 hours by a wallet that will be added to my backend private allowlist. The reason I've decided to do it this way is for a few reasons.

    1. This allows me to choose who gets to mint. While some people in this space will be up in arms about it, saying it's not fair, it's not decentralized, or that I'm a scammer. Alls I have to say is, go fuck yourself. I don't need your approval to do it this way and I could give two fucks about how you feel.
    2. This will prevent the project from being bot-ed. One of my biggest concerns when I started building Phil was the thought of having a couple people being able to bot the project, making it a dumpster fire from the start, taking away from the purpose and intent of Phil.
    3. This is a free mint, and is something that I've put countless hours into building, refining, and making sure that I'm proud of. So I'm sorry that I'm not sorry if your feelings get hurt because you didn't get a chance to mint one, or weren't "chosen". As a good friend in the space has stated "It's not my help that you need".

    3(a). Every 36th Phil will be auctioned, so this will allow for absolutely anyone to potentially win a chance to mint a Phil. The auction will end at the 36 hour 9 minute mark to the highest bidder. The bidder will then be able to mint a Phil at any point during the project's duration. The benefit of this compared to the other mints will be the ability to mint anytime during the duration of the project, where the chosen minters will only be able to mint during that 36 hour window they were chosen for.

    There's more to be said, and in due time it all will be, but for right now this is a good place to start. More details soon.
